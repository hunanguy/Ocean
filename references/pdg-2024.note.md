**What the reviews give.**
1. Sec. 93: charge quantization by anomaly cancellation; the cancellation of all triangle anomalies; hypercharge quantized when embedded in a unified group.
2. Sec. 93: the running of the three gauge couplings and their near-meeting at high energy (unification fails in the standard model alone, works in its supersymmetric extension, M_G ~ 2 × 10¹⁶ GeV).
3. Sec. 93: the seesaw, neutrino masses from heavy singlet fermions integrated out, with a scale M ~ 10¹⁴ GeV.
4. Sec. 10.7: the parameter ρ₀ (Eqs. 10.86, 10.88) is 1 for the standard model's Higgs doublet and is shifted by Higgs multiplets of weak isospin above ½ with significant vacuum values; the data hold such contributions to less than a few percent.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** These are the standard model's results for the same fifteen pieces per family; the framework uses them, and gives its own reasons for the inputs (§12.8, §12.9).
