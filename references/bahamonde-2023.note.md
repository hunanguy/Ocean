**What the paper gives.**
1. The torsion scalar of the teleparallel equivalent of general relativity (TEGR): T = ¼ T¹ + ½ T² − T³, with T¹ = T^{μνρ}T_{μνρ}, T² = T^{μνρ}T_{ρνμ}, T³ = T^μ_{μρ}T_ν^{νρ} (Eqs. 4.158, 5.4–5.5, 5.17).
2. The Ricci scalar differs from it by a boundary term, R = −T + B, "which guarantees the dynamical equivalence between the Einstein-Hilbert and TEGR action"; the field equations "organically admit all solutions that appear in GR" (pp. 18, 69–70).
3. The general quadratic family L = c₁T¹ + c₂T² + c₃T³ (new general relativity); its antisymmetric part, the response to the frame's orientation (the spin connection), vanishes identically only when 2c₁ − c₂ = 2c₂ + c₃ = 0, i.e. for multiples of TEGR (Eq. 5.23; p. 185: "the family of Lagrangians satisfying these conditions are multiples of TEGR").
4. The three-parameter family generally contains ghosts; matter coupled to the metric keeps the weak equivalence principle (pp. 19, 79).
5. The boundary term matters where spacetime boundaries do (black-hole entropy, Casimir effect, Hamiltonian formulation) (p. 19).
