# Kamenshchik, Moschella & Pasquier 2001 — verification note

**Citation.** A. Kamenshchik, U. Moschella and V. Pasquier, "An alternative to quintessence", Phys. Lett. B 511, 265 (2001) (volume and page confirmed by Bento et al. 2002 and Sandvik et al. 2004); arXiv:gr-qc/0103004. Read in full 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv (8 pp.). Full text not stored.

**Cited in 3.12.** §11.6, The current through time (with Bento et al. 2002, as the generalized Chaplygin pressure–density law).

**In 3.12.** Cited as a law the step does not need: the Ocean is stiff, so near a mass its rulers and clocks change, not its amount, and no pressure–density law enters. The point for W2 below does not arise in 3.12.

**Claims, and what the paper says.**

1. *The Chaplygin gas, p = −A/ρ, behaves as dust at high density and as a cosmological constant at low density.* — Supported: ρ = √(A + B/a⁶), dust-like at small a, de Sitter at large a, with a stiff-matter intermediate phase. Here ρ is *energy* density.
2. *Generalization to other exponents.* — The paper notes p = −Cρ^(−α), with α ≥ −1, gives a similar evolution. So an exponent α = 3 is admitted by this generalization as written, though not studied.

**Point for W2.** The Chaplygin law relates pressure to energy density. W2's Bernoulli step uses the Ocean's density n (= ρ² in 3.8's notation, where ρ is the modulus). W2 must say which density its law refers to before claiming the correspondence.
