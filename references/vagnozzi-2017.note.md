# Vagnozzi 2017, "Recovering a MOND-like acceleration law in mimetic gravity" — verification note

**Citation.** S. Vagnozzi, "Recovering a MOND-like acceleration law in mimetic gravity", arXiv:1708.00603 (Class. Quantum Grav. 34 (2017) 185006, from recollection).

**Read.** 6 Nov 2026, arXiv PDF: the action, the weak-field derivation (Eqs. 6–33) and caveats.

**What the paper gives.** A matter coupling γJ^μ∂_μφ to the mimetic field gives non-geodesic motion with an extra force; with it taken parallel to the total acceleration, a ≈ √(a₀ a_N) in the outer parts, with a₀ set empirically at ~10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² and carrying an r-dependence; an ideas paper with no fits to data.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** Not usable for the eddy's limit: the coupling acts on matter's current, so different patterns would respond differently, against §11.2 (every pattern responds alike), and a₀ is put in by hand. Recorded so the route is not tried again.

**Status.** Read at the parts listed; not supportive; not cited.
