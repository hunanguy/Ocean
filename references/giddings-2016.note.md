# Giddings 2016 — verification note

**Citation.** S. B. Giddings, "Hawking radiation, the Stefan–Boltzmann law, and unitarization", Phys. Lett. B 754, 39 (2016). Read from arXiv:1511.08221v3 (12 Jan 2016), full text, all 9 pages.

**Cited by.** B3 (§8.1).

**Claims our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *The radiation emerges from a region comparable in size to the black hole, not from a glowing surface.* — Supported. Conclusion: "the source of Hawking radiation is a quantum region of size ∆r ∼ R outside the black hole horizon", the "atmosphere". The effective emitting radius from the high-frequency cross section is R_a = (3√3/2)R ≈ 2.6 R (eq. 7). The stress-tensor analysis shows the outgoing flux "vanishes at the horizon; the proper outward flux builds up from there, over a range of r ∼ R".
2. *Its typical wavelength is several times the horizon radius.* — **Wrong in our text.** Eq. (17): λ_T = 2π/T = 8π²R ≈ 79 R, where R is the Schwarzschild radius. The typical wavelength is about eighty times the horizon radius, not several. Correct B3 to "about eighty times the horizon radius".
3. *A pattern falling freely detects nothing at the horizon, while one held stationary near it detects a thermal bath.* — Consistent. The paper describes detectors at constant r as "properly accelerating", experiencing "the Unruh effect with a temperature that is related to Hawking's by a blueshift"; footnote 1 reports Unruh's point about "the failure of infalling observers to detect particles near the horizon". The infalling half rests on Unruh 1977 (Phys. Rev. D 15, 365), not on Giddings; cite that too, or keep Giddings for the atmosphere claim only.

**Note on framing.** Giddings works within standard semiclassical gravity and argues that unitarity requires modifying Hawking radiation. Our C1 (preservation of the state) is compatible with his position that unitarity holds; we should not imply he endorses the Ocean.

**Mismatches.** One: the wavelength factor (claim 2). Fixed in the proposal on 18 October.
