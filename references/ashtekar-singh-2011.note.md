# Ashtekar & Singh 2011, "Loop Quantum Cosmology: A Status Report" — verification note

**Citation.** A. Ashtekar and P. Singh, "Loop quantum cosmology: a status report", Class. Quantum Grav. 28 (2011) 213001; arXiv:1108.0893. Journal reference confirmed (ADS 2011CQGra..28u3001A; Penn State record).

**Read.** 6 Nov 2026 via alphaXiv, pages 1, 40, 73–75, 81, 99, 121. Not read in full.

**What the paper gives.**
1. Section V B, Eq. (5.7): the modified Friedmann equation of loop quantum cosmology, H² = (8πG/3) ρ (1 − ρ/ρ_max), with ρ_max = 3/(8πGγ²λ²) ≈ 0.41 ρ_Planck, set by the area gap of loop quantum gravity (γ the Barbero–Immirzi parameter). The universe bounces where ρ = ρ_max.
2. Eq. (5.9): Ḣ = −4πG(ρ + P)(1 − 2ρ/ρ_max); the conservation law of matter is unchanged (5.10).
3. The origin is quantum geometry: the effective constraint replaces b² by sin²(λb)/λ²; "quantum geometry creates a brand new repulsive force".
4. Section V E 2: the same effective dynamics applied to the ekpyrotic/cyclic model, resolving its crunch.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** The paper's illustrative form for the loosening grip, G_eff = G(1 − ρ/ρ_c) with H² = (8πG/3)ρ(1 − ρ/ρ_c) (§11.9, Appendix A), is exactly this equation. In loop quantum cosmology it comes from quantum geometry and ρ_c is fixed near the Planck density by the area gap; in the Ocean the grip κ(φ) is the medium's own (the premise that the coupling depends on the local state), and only the form is borrowed. Cited as the source of the form, not as support for the Ocean's mechanism.
