# LEP measurements of the strong force's colour factors

**Correction (2 November 2026):** the summary arXiv:hep-ex/0406058 is by S. Bethke, not Kluth: S. Bethke, "QCD studies at LEP", Phys. Rep. 403–404, 203 (2004); doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2004.08.014. Re-read 2 November 2026 via alphaXiv (pp. 1–5, 20). Its §5 gives the combined C_A = 2.89 ± 0.01 (stat) ± 0.21 (syst), C_F = 1.30 ± 0.01 ± 0.09 (from Kluth's QCD 03 combination, its ref. [56]), in agreement with SU(3) (C_A = 3, C_F = 4/3), ruling out an abelian vector gluon; §6 gives C_A/C_F = 2.22 ± 0.11 from DELPHI's multiplicities. The ratio of the combined values, 2.89/1.30 = 2.22 ± 0.22 (errors combined), is the figure the paper uses. Cited in 3.13 as `bethke2004` (§12.8, §12.9).

Read 29 Oct 2026 (abstracts / summary sections):
- Combined values above (hep-ex/0406058, §5).
- ALEPH (Heister et al.): C_A = 2.93 ± 0.14 ± 0.58, C_F = 1.35 ± 0.07 ± 0.26 (summary in arXiv:hep-ex/0410064).
- OPAL 2001 (Abbiendi et al., EPJC 20, 601; arXiv:hep-ex/0101044): C_A = 3.02 ± 0.25 ± 0.49, C_F = 1.34 ± 0.13 ± 0.22.
- OPAL 1995 (Akers et al., Z. Phys. C 65, 367): C_A/C_F = 2.11 ± 0.16 ± 0.28.
- Seymour review, arXiv:hep-ph/9411361 §5.3: four-jet fits are consistent with SU(3); the only other groups with three quark colours, SO(3) and an abelian toy model, are excluded.
- Use: claude/arrows_direction.py. Three real arrows mixed by turning (SO(3)) give C_A/C_F = 1; measured 2.22 ± 0.22.
