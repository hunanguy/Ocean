# Sandvik, Tegmark, Zaldarriaga & Waga 2004 — verification note

**Citation.** H. B. Sandvik, M. Tegmark, M. Zaldarriaga and I. Waga, "The end of unified dark matter?", Phys. Rev. D 69, 123524 (2004); arXiv:astro-ph/0212114. Journal reference confirmed 22 October 2026: Bento, Bertolami & Sen (arXiv:astro-ph/0407239, ref. [13]) cite it as Phys. Rev. D69 (2004) 123524. Read in full 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv (5 pp.); arXiv v2 PDF supplied by Keith 22 October. Full text not stored.

**Cited by.** W2 (caveat), R31.

**What the paper finds.**

1. *Unified dark-matter models with pressure a unique function of density produce oscillations or exponential blow-up of the matter power spectrum.* For the generalized Chaplygin gas the 2dF data give −8.1 × 10⁻⁷ < α < 7.9 × 10⁻⁶; 99.999 per cent of the previously allowed range is excluded.
2. *Generality.* Any such model is excluded if |d ln p/d ln ρ| ≳ 10⁻⁵ where pressure matters, because the sound-speed term (k c_s/aH)² is enormous on galactic scales.
3. *The escape.* The authors note the idea survives only if pressure is *not* uniquely determined by density, for example through a second field on which p depends, so the effective sound speed differs from the adiabatic one.

**Consequence for W2 (sharper than the proposal said).** W2 needs c_s ∝ n⁻², i.e. |d ln p/d ln n| ~ 1. If that same single-variable law were the Ocean's cosmological equation of state, it would be excluded by about five orders of magnitude. W2 survives only if the Ocean's pressure is not a function of one density alone. 3.8 already gives the Ocean two state variables (the amount ρ and the phase rate μ, §2.2), which is exactly the escape the authors name; it must be shown to work. The proposal's line "the same law governs P(φ) and the Heartbeat" is withdrawn in that form.
