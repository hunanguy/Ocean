# Hahn 1950 — verification note

**Citation.** E. L. Hahn, "Spin Echoes", Phys. Rev. 80, 580–594 (1950); received 22 May 1950. DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.80.580. Read in full from the scan supplied by the user (OCR layer, 15 journal pages).

**Cited in 3.12.** §9.2, Nothing is destroyed (spin echoes: what has not been truly randomized returns).

**What the paper shows.**
- Nuclear spins in a liquid are tipped by a short radio-frequency pulse and then precess freely. Because the magnet's field is slightly uneven across the sample, each group of spins turns at its own Larmor frequency. They fan out, interfere destructively, and the signal disappears: "all moment vectors interfere destructively with one another and distribute themselves isotropically".
- A second pulse at time τ flips their positions. Each keeps precessing forward at its own rate, and at time 2τ they arrive back in step and produce the echo: "the phase differences of all moment vectors … are effectively cancelled at the time of the echo maximum. This cancellation is made possible by the second pulse."
- What does not come back is set by genuine relaxation. The spin–spin time T₂ and spin–lattice time T₁ come from interactions with neighbours. Self-diffusion of the molecules through the uneven field destroys phase memory, giving an extra decay of exp(−kt³/3). Hahn uses the echo's decay to measure these quantities.
- Hahn remarks on "applying echo patterns as a type of memory device".

**Claims our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *Earlier draft: "Recovery of a scrambled pattern need not mean reversal, as the spin echo shows for simple systems."* — Right in spirit, but loose. In Hahn's account nothing runs backward: the spins continue forward, and the pulse rearranges them so that forward motion brings them back into step. Many later treatments call this an effective time reversal of the dephasing. So C1 now says exactly what the paper shows: rephasing by forward motion after a single pulse. It also states the limit Hahn measures: what diffusion and spin–spin interactions have truly randomized does not return.
2. *Fit to the framework.* — The echo separates two kinds of disorder. Apparent disorder that keeps its detailed state (the dephased spins) can be recovered. Disorder in which the information has spread into the environment (diffusion, relaxation) cannot be recovered by the same means. That matches C1's distinction between coarse-grained disorder rising and the fine-grained state being preserved.
