# ALPHA-g 2023 — verification note

**Citation.** E. K. Anderson et al. (ALPHA Collaboration), "Observation of the effect of gravity on the motion of antimatter", Nature 621, 716–722 (2023); published online 27 September 2023. DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06527-1. Open access, CC BY 4.0. Read in full from the PDF supplied by the user (23 pages including Methods and Extended Data).

**Cited by.** D5 (§10.4(5), matter and antimatter).

**Claims our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *Earlier draft: "antihydrogen falls with an acceleration consistent with g".* — Supported, and now stated more exactly. Antihydrogen atoms were released from a vertical magnetic trap while the barriers at top and bottom were lowered over 20 s; the fraction escaping downward was measured for imposed magnetic biases from −3g to +3g and compared with simulations. Result: the acceleration "is directed towards the Earth and has magnitude a_g = (0.75 ± 0.13 (statistical + systematic) ± 0.16 (simulation))g", "consistent with a downward gravitational acceleration of 1g". The chance of a result this extreme with no gravity acting is 2.9 × 10⁻⁴. Repulsive gravity of 1g is ruled out: its probability is "less than 10⁻¹⁵". D5 now gives the value and the exclusion.
2. *Precision.* The authors say outright that "our goal here is not to make a precision determination of the magnitude", only to fix the sign and the scale of the effect. The largest uncertainty is the off-axis magnetic field model, worth 0.16g. So D5 should not present this as a precision test of the equivalence principle for antimatter; it is the first direct observation of the sign.

**Related points useful elsewhere.**
- The paper states "the theoretical consensus is that any laboratory mass must be attracted by the Earth", and that the results "do not support cosmological models relying on repulsive matter–antimatter gravitation".
- This bears directly on the Ocean reading. If antimatter is the same pattern with the opposite circulation sense, it must respond to a gradient of the Ocean's state exactly as matter does. This measurement is consistent with that, and it rules out any version in which the opposite circulation sense reverses the response.
