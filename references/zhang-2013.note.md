# Zhang, Rix, van de Ven, Bovy, Liu & Zhao 2013, the vertical force near the Sun from SEGUE K dwarfs — verification note

**Citation.** L. Zhang, H.-W. Rix, G. van de Ven, J. Bovy, C. Liu and G. Zhao, "The Gravitational Potential Near the Sun From SEGUE K-dwarf Kinematics", Astrophys. J. 772, 108 (2013); arXiv:1209.0256 (v2, 31 May 2013). Read 21 November 2026 via alphaXiv: pp. 1–2, 4–10, 13, 15–22, 27, Table 1 (p. 46).

**Used by.** Lisanti et al. 2019 and 2023 take their vertical data from this paper; item 2 of the 3.16 list.

**What the paper gives.**
1. 9,157 SEGUE K dwarfs split into three abundance populations: metal-rich ([Fe/H] −0.5 to 0.3, [α/Fe] 0–0.15; 3,672 stars), intermediate (−1.0 to −0.3, 0.15–0.25; 1,416), metal-poor (−1.5 to −0.5, 0.25–0.50; 2,001).
2. Vertical number densities and velocity dispersions in bins of |z| from about 0.3 to 1.2 kpc at the Sun's radius (dispersions 17–26, 25–37 and 35–42 km/s), fitted with the vertical Jeans equation. The binned values are shown only in Figs. 6 and 7, not tabulated.
3. Total surface density within 1.0 kpc 67 ± 6 M☉/pc², of which stars 42 ± 5 (gas fixed at 13); local dark-matter density 0.0065 ± 0.0023 M☉/pc³; tracer scale heights about 260, 460 and 850–910 pc.

**Status.** Read and verified 21 November 2026. The data points themselves were not available as text.
