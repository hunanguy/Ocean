# Lim, Sawicki & Vikman 2010, "Dust of Dark Energy" — verification note

**Citation.** E. A. Lim, I. Sawicki and A. Vikman, "Dust of Dark Energy", JCAP 05 (2010) 012; arXiv:1003.5751. Journal reference from recollection; the paper cites the arXiv number.

**Read.** 6 Nov 2026 via the ar5iv HTML (arXiv:1003.5751): action, constraint, energy and pressure, perturbations and the closing open questions. Not read in full.

**What the paper gives.**
1. Action S = ∫√−g [K(φ, X) + λ(X − ½μ²(φ))], X = ½(∇φ)², λ a multiplier with no kinetic term. Varying λ fixes the norm of ∇φ: φ̇ = μ(φ) along u_α = μ⁻¹∇_αφ.
2. The flow is geodesic (four-acceleration zero) even though the pressure is not zero.
3. Energy ε = μ²(K_X + λ) − K; pressure p = K(φ, ½μ²(φ)), a function of the clock field alone; ε̇ + θ(ε + p) = 0.
4. No wave-like modes: sound speed exactly zero on all backgrounds.
5. Open, as stated by the authors: caustics; whether such fluids form static, stable halos; the origin of the initial conditions; Hamiltonian structure and quantization; strong coupling; phantom cases.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** The phase's law in the build's terms (Eq. 38) has this structure with K = v²μ² − V₀ and μ fixed: the settled Ocean is w = −1, a departure is dust carried with the flow, and the phase has no wave of its own. Precedent for the form; cited as such in §12.7.
