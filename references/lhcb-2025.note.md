# LHCb Collaboration 2025 — verification note

**Citation.** R. Aaij et al. (LHCb Collaboration), "Observation of charge–parity symmetry breaking in baryon decays", Nature 643, 1223 (2025); arXiv:2503.16954; CERN-EP-2025-031. Open access, CC BY 4.0. Read from the arXiv version dated 14 August 2025, full text including Methods and references (author list skimmed).

**Cited by.** D5 (§10.4(5)).

**Claims our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *CP violation is seen in kaons, B and charm mesons and, in 2025, baryons.* — Supported. Introduction: CP violation "was first observed in strange-meson decays in 1964", "later also observed in beauty-meson decays in 2001 and in charm-meson decays in 2019". This paper reports "the first observation of CP violation in baryon decays": in Λb⁰ → pK⁻π⁺π⁻ the asymmetry is A_CP = (2.45 ± 0.46 ± 0.10)%, 5.2σ from zero, from about 9 fb⁻¹ of 2011–2018 data. The largest local asymmetry, 6.0σ, is in the region dominated by excited-nucleon resonances.
2. *[CP violation is] too small in the Standard Model to explain the excess of matter.* — Supported. Introduction: "the amount of matter-antimatter asymmetry explained by the CKM mechanism is vastly smaller than what astronomical observations indicate."
3. *The one measured difference between matter and antimatter is CP violation in the weak interaction.* — Consistent. The paper attributes Standard Model CP violation to the CKM mechanism of the weak interaction. "The one measured difference" is our framing, not the paper's.
