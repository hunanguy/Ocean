# Lange et al. 2021 — verification note

**Citation.** R. Lange, N. Huntemann, J. M. Rahm, C. Sanner, H. Shao, B. Lipphardt, Chr. Tamm, S. Weyers and E. Peik, "Improved limits for violations of local position invariance from atomic clock comparisons", Phys. Rev. Lett. 126, 011102 (2021). Read from arXiv:2010.06620v2 (7 Jan 2021), full text.

**Cited in 3.12.** §7.6, Consequences (atomic clocks: drift of the proton-to-electron mass ratio, a few parts in 10¹⁷ per year).

**Claim our text makes.** *Particle-mass ratios are constrained to about 10⁻¹⁷ per year by atomic clocks.*

**What the paper says.** Abstract and Table II: the proton-to-electron mass ratio μ = m_p/m_e has fractional temporal variation (1/μ)(dμ/dt) = −8(36) × 10⁻¹⁸ per year, consistent with zero; a factor of about 2 better than the previous limits. (The fine-structure constant is limited to 1.0(1.1) × 10⁻¹⁸ per year.) All results are "consistent with zero to within less than 1.3 standard deviations".

**Assessment.** The one-standard-deviation uncertainty is 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁷ per year. "About 10⁻¹⁷ per year" understates it slightly. Revised E4 wording: "to a few parts in 10¹⁷ per year by atomic clocks". Note that the μ limit depends on assumptions about the drift of X_q = m_q/Λ_QCD taken from Guéna et al. 2012.

**Mismatches.** Minor wording only (fixed 18 October).

**Status.** Verified 18 October 2026.
