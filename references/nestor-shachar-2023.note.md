# Nestor Shachar et al. 2023, RC100: rotation curves of 100 massive star-forming galaxies at z = 0.6–2.5 — verification note

**Citation.** A. Nestor Shachar, S. H. Price, N. M. Förster Schreiber, R. Genzel et al., "RC100: Rotation Curves of 100 Massive Star-Forming Galaxies at z = 0.6–2.5 Reveal Little Dark Matter on Galactic Scales", arXiv:2209.12199 (v1). Astrophys. J. 944, 78 (2023); journal reference confirmed 2 November 2026 (ADS 2023ApJ...944...78N). Read 1 November 2026 via alphaXiv: abstract (p. 1), pp. 3, 9, 12–15, 18–20, 22, 26.

**Cited by.** `claude/eddy-strength.md`, "The capacity g†". Not cited in the paper.

**What the paper gives.**
1. 100 Hα/CO rotation curves to ~2 R_e (R_e ~ 5 kpc), forward-modelled with bulge, disk, halo and pressure support by three methods that agree (pp. 1, 9, 22).
2. Median dark-matter share within R_e: 0.38 ± 0.23 at z ~ 1, 0.27 ± 0.18 at z ~ 2; fit ⟨f_DM⟩ = 0.75 (1 + z)^−1.07, from 0.38 at z = 0.85 to 0.17 at z = 2.44 (pp. 1, 13, 22).
3. f_DM anticorrelates strongly with baryon surface density within R_e (Spearman −0.84 at z ~ 1, −0.64 at z ~ 2) (p. 14).
4. Per-galaxy table in Appendix B (p. 26 on): z, M*, M_baryon, M_bulge, R_e, f_DM, V_c(R_e), σ₀. Not yet extracted.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** A fixed capacity gives an eddy share that falls as baryon surface density rises and does not rise with redshift; a capacity following H(z) would raise the share at high z, the opposite of the measured trend. Galaxy-by-galaxy test against Appendix B not done.
