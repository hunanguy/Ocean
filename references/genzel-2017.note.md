# Genzel et al. 2017, strongly baryon-dominated disks at z ≈ 0.9–2.4 — verification note

**Citation.** R. Genzel, N. M. Förster Schreiber, H. Übler, P. Lang, T. Naab et al., "Strongly baryon-dominated disk galaxies at the peak of galaxy formation ten billion years ago", Nature 543, 397 (2017); arXiv:1703.04310 (v1). Journal reference confirmed 2 November 2026 (ADS 2017Natur.543..397G). Read 1 November 2026 via alphaXiv: pp. 1–2, 4–5 (text, Fig. 2, Table 1), Methods pp. 8, 12–13, 15.

**Cited by.** `claude/eddy-strength.md`, "The capacity g†"; script `claude/eddy_capacity.py`. Not cited in the paper.

**What the paper gives.**
1. Outer Hα rotation curves of six massive star-forming disks (log M* ≈ 10.6–11.1, R₁/₂ ≈ 4–9 kpc) at z = 0.854–2.383 fall beyond the peak, to v(R_out)/v_max ≈ 0.3–0.9; a stack of 97 further disks confirms falling curves are common (pp. 1–2).
2. Table 1 fit values (bulge + n = 1 disk + NFW halo, pressure-corrected, constant dispersion): z; M_baryon (10¹¹ M☉) 1.7, 2.3, 1.0, 1.7, 1.7, 2.1; bulge share 0.2, 0.35, 0.4, 0.6, 0.37, 0.15; R₁/₂ (kpc) 7.3, 7.4, 4.9, 5.5, 3.3, 6.0; f_DM(R₁/₂) 0.21 (±0.1), 0.17 (< 0.38), 0.19 (±0.09), 0.0 (< 0.08), 0.0 (< 0.07), 0.12 (< 0.26), with ±2 rms ranges (p. 5). Order: COS4 01351, D3a 6397, GS4 43501, zC 406690, zC 400569, D3a 15504.
3. Bulge radius fixed at 1 kpc; disk scale length R₁/₂/1.68 (Methods p. 12).
4. Caveats stated by the authors: the six were picked for falling curves already seen (p. 15); a dispersion rising outward would leave room for more dark matter at and beyond R₁/₂ (p. 12).

**Bearing on the Ocean.** With the eddy set by baryonic mass and capacity alone, a fixed capacity fits 5–6 of the 6 dark-matter shares; a capacity following H(z) (×3.25–3.5 at z > 2) fails all three z > 2 galaxies. The capacity at z ≈ 2.2–2.4 is at most 1.0–2.7 times today's.
