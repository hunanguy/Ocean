# DES supernova releases: DES-SN5YR (2024) and DES-Dovekie (2026)

**Citations.** DES Collaboration (T. M. C. Abbott et al.; the release README calls it Vincenzi et al. 2024), "The Dark Energy Survey: Cosmology Results With ~1500 New High-redshift Type Ia Supernovae Using The Full 5-year Dataset", arXiv:2401.02929 (2024) (DES-SN5YR). B. Popovic et al., "The Dark Energy Survey Supernova Program: A Reanalysis Of Cosmology Results And Evidence For Evolving Dark Energy With An Updated Type Ia Supernova Calibration", arXiv:2511.07517 (2026). Data: github.com/des-science/DES-SN5YR (Keith pointed to it, 22 Nov 2026 21:38): main branch is DES-Dovekie ("supercedes the original DES-SN5YR results from Vincenzi et al. 2024, which is still available on Zenodo and this tagged version", tag 1.3). Read 22 Nov 2026 21:59 from the arXiv abstract pages (for the 3.16 citation). DES 2024 (DES Collaboration, T. M. C. Abbott et al.): 1,635 DES type Ia supernovae (0.10 < z < 1.13) with 194 at low redshift; supernovae alone give Ω_M = 0.352 ± 0.017 in flat ΛCDM, and the wCDM, w0waCDM and combined fits are consistent with a cosmological constant within about 2σ. Popovic et al. 2026 (doi:10.1093/mnras/stag632): recalibration (cross-calibration, white-dwarf links to low-redshift surveys, retrained SALT3, a fix to the host colour law); about 1,600 DES and 200 low-redshift supernovae; Ω_m = 0.330 ± 0.015 (−0.022 from DES-SN5YR); with the microwave background and DESI DR2, w0 = −0.803 ± 0.054, wa = −0.72 ± 0.21; evidence against a cosmological constant drops from 4.2σ to 3.2σ, which the authors call a weak preference. Cited in the 3.16 draft, §5.1, as des2024 and popovic2026; DES 2024's journal reference not confirmed from a read source, so arXiv only. Used for `claude/dynamo-output.md`.

**DES-SN5YR, tag 1.3.** `4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/DES-SN5YR_HD.csv` (CID, IDSURVEY, zCMB, zHD, zHEL, MU, MUERR_FINAL; 1,829 supernovae) and `STAT+SYS.txt.gz` (first value 1829, then the systematic covariance; the release's likelihood, `5_COSMOLOGY/SN_only_cosmosis_likelihood.py`, adds MUERR_FINAL² on the diagonal). Theory μ = 5 log₁₀((1 + zCMB)(1 + zHEL) D_A(zCMB)) + 25, offset marginalized analytically (the code cites arXiv:astro-ph/0104009, eq. A9–12).

**DES-Dovekie, main.** `4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/DES-Dovekie_HD.csv` (VARNAMES: CID IDSURVEY zHD zHEL MU MUERR MUERR_VPEC MUERR_SYS PROBIA_BEAMS; 1,820 supernovae; "MU (assuming H0 of 70)") and `STAT+SYS.npz` (the README: "the Covariance Matrices provided here are _inverse_ covariance matrices", stored as the upper triangle; unpacked as in `DES-Dovekie-SN_Likelihood.py`). Global parameters quoted in the README: α = 0.169, β = 3.14, γ = 0.033, M₀ = −29.96210.

**Held-fixed fits with DESI DR2 BAO (flat, w = −1):** Ω_m 0.310 (2024), 0.306 (Dovekie). Two-number formula with DESI DR2 BAO: w0 = −0.78, wa = −0.73 (2024); w0 = −0.84, wa = −0.54 (Dovekie).
