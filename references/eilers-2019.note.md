# Eilers, Hogg, Rix & Ness 2019, the Milky Way's circular velocity curve — verification note

**Citation.** A.-C. Eilers, D. W. Hogg, H.-W. Rix and M. K. Ness, "The Circular Velocity Curve of the Milky Way from 5 to 25 kpc", Astrophys. J. 871, 120 (2019); arXiv:1810.09466 (v2, 4 December 2018). Journal reference from recollection; confirm before citing. Read 21 November 2026 via alphaXiv: pp. 1–13 (§§1–6, Table 1, Figs. 1–4).

**Used by.** Item 2 of the 3.16 list, the vertical test (`claude/item2-vertical/`).

**What the paper gives.**
1. Circular velocity from 5 to 25 kpc from 23,129 red giants (APOGEE DR14 + Gaia DR2 + 2MASS + WISE, spectrophotometric parallaxes about 10%), Jeans equation in an axisymmetric potential; |z| ≤ 0.5 kpc or within 6° of the plane, [α/Fe] < 0.12.
2. v_c(R⊙) = 229.0 ± 0.2 km/s, declining at −1.7 ± 0.1 km/s/kpc (systematic 0.46); systematic 2–5% out to 20 kpc, ≲ 3% at the Sun. R⊙ = 8.122 ± 0.031 kpc (GRAVITY 2018).
3. Table 1: 38 values of v_c with σ− and σ+ from 5.27 to 24.82 kpc; the first 26 (to 17.75 kpc) are the ones Lisanti et al. 2023 use, and are copied into `mw_data.py`.

**Status.** Read and verified 21 November 2026.
