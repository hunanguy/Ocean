# Will 2014 — verification note

**Citation.** C. M. Will, "The confrontation between general relativity and experiment", Living Rev. Relativ. 17, 4 (2014); arXiv:1403.7377. Journal reference confirmed 22 October 2026: the arXiv record gives DOI 10.12942/lrr-2014-4. Read 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv: contents, §3.2 (PPN tables and metric, pp. 29, 31), §4.1–4.3 (pp. 40, 42–45, 48), §4.4 (pp. 51–52). Full text not stored. Used in place of Bertotti, Iess & Tortora 2003 (Nature, not on arXiv), whose Cassini result it reports.

**Cited by.** W1, W2, R31.

**Claims, and what the paper says.**

1. *PPN metric:* g₀₀ = −1 + 2U − 2βU² + …, g_ij = (1 + 2γU)δ_ij. — Supported (Box 2). W2's |g₀₀| = 1 − 2u + 2βu² and g_ij = 1 + 2γu match.
2. *Light deflection is ½(1 + γ) times the GR value; the equivalence principle alone gives the first half, space curvature the second.* — Supported (§4.1.1, eq. 59 and discussion). This is the basis of W1's even split; it stands without Dicke.
3. *Cassini:* γ − 1 = (2.1 ± 2.3) × 10⁻⁵. — Supported (§4.1.2).
4. *Perihelion advance ∝ (2 + 2γ − β)/3.* — Supported (eq. 64). W2's linear-deficit figure (7/6, about 17 per cent too fast) follows.
5. *β:* β − 1 = (−4.1 ± 7.8) × 10⁻⁵ from Mercury (Messenger) with Cassini's γ; Table 4 lists |β − 1| < 8 × 10⁻⁵. — Supported. R31's "10⁻⁴ level or better" holds.
6. *Preferred-frame parameters.* Table 4: α₁ ≲ 10⁻⁴ (lunar laser ranging) and 7 × 10⁻⁵ (PSR J1738+0333); α₂ ≲ 2 × 10⁻⁹ (millisecond pulsars; strictly strong-field analogues). — **Correction:** W2 and the v3.8 proposal's F3 quoted "10⁵–10⁷". The current figures are α₁ ≲ 10⁻⁴ and α₂ ≲ 2 × 10⁻⁹.

**Status.** Verified 21 October 2026, with the preferred-frame figures corrected. Journal reference confirmed 22 October 2026.
