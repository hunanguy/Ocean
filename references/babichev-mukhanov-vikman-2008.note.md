# Babichev, Mukhanov & Vikman 2008 — verification note

**Citation.** E. Babichev, V. Mukhanov and A. Vikman, "k-Essence, superluminal propagation, causality and emergent geometry", JHEP 0802, 101 (2008); arXiv:0708.0561. Journal reference confirmed 22 October 2026 from the arXiv record (JHEP 0802:101, 2008; DOI 10.1088/1126-6708/2008/02/101). Read 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv: §2 (pp. 4–7), §3 (pp. 8–10), §4 (p. 12), §7–8 (p. 21), appendices A, B, D (pp. 23, 25–26, 29). Full text not stored.

**Cited in 3.12.** §11.4, Why the minimal law cannot carry the split (the disformal acoustic metric in which the ripple of the phase propagates, with Visser 1998).

**Claims, and what the paper says.**

1. *Perturbations of a field with Lagrangian L(X) propagate in an emergent metric* G⁻¹_μν = (L_X/c_s)[g_μν − c_s²(L_XX/L_X)∂_μφ∂_νφ], *with c_s² = (1 + 2X L_XX/L_X)⁻¹.* — Supported (their eqs. 2.6, 2.12; signature +−−−, X = ½(∂φ)²). With u_μ = ∂_μφ/√(2X) this is (L_X/c_s)[g − (1 − c_s²)uu], which in mostly-plus signature is N3's Ω²[g + (1 − c_s²)uu] with Ω² = L_X/c_s. N3's c_s² and Ω² checked against these, allowing for N3's X being twice theirs (a constant factor, irrelevant). Matches.
2. *Hydrodynamic form.* — Appendix D gives the same metric in fluid variables and attributes it to Moncrief 1980.
3. *Localized configurations in the trivial vacuum never outrun the background metric's light cone* (their §7). — Bears on N4: in a law written on η, short-scale structure keeps η's cone.

**Mismatches.** None for N3's formulas.
