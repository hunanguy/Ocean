# Clowe et al. 2006 — A direct empirical proof of the existence of dark matter
D. Clowe, M. Bradač, A. H. Gonzalez, M. Markevitch, S. W. Randall, C. Jones and D. Zaritsky, *A direct empirical proof of the existence of dark matter*, arXiv:astro-ph/0608407 (v1, 19 Aug 2006; "ApJ Letters in press"). Read in full 22 November 2026. Journal volume and page not confirmed from a read source; cited by arXiv number.

**What it says that we use.**
- The merging cluster 1E0657−558 (the "Bullet Cluster", z = 0.296): two galaxy concentrations 0.72 Mpc apart; each has its X-ray plasma offset toward the center, slowed by ram pressure, while the galaxies passed through; the cores passed through each other about 100 Myr ago, the subcluster moving away at about 4,700 km/s.
- Weak-lensing maps "show that the gravitational potential does not trace the plasma distribution, the dominant baryonic mass component, but rather approximately traces the distribution of galaxies"; both lensing peaks are offset about 8σ from their plasma clouds and lie within 1σ of the luminosity centroid of their galaxies.
- Table 2 (100 kpc apertures): main cluster, at the brightest galaxy plasma 5.5 and stars 0.54 (10¹² M☉), κ̄ 0.36; at the plasma peak 6.6 and 0.23, κ̄ 0.05; subcluster 2.7 and 0.58, κ̄ 0.20, against 5.8 and 0.12, κ̄ 0.02. Plasma and stellar masses are measured directly from the X-ray and optical images (stars with M/L_I = 2, uncertain between 0.5 and 3).
- Lensing ratio of about 7:1 between the galaxy-plus-unseen component and the plasma; the absolute lensing levels match clusters of similar temperature whose gas and galaxies coincide.
- "Any non-standard gravitational force that scales with baryonic mass will fail to reproduce these observations. The lensing peaks require unseen matter concentrations that are more massive than and offset from the plasma."
- Two other merging clusters, MS1054−03 and A520, show similar offsets (lower resolution).

**Bearing on the Ocean.** The eddy is not a force law: it is Ocean whose builds carry more than settled Ocean, and its energy is load, so it lenses. The observation constrains which matter an eddy answers to: an eddy built by Equation 15 from all the ordinary matter inside, mostly plasma in a cluster, would be centered on the plasma; the lensing follows the galaxies. Eddies that travel with their galaxies fit the offset in kind; whether they carry enough is open (3.16, §13.3 "Galaxy clusters").
