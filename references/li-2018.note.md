# Li, Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2018 — Fitting the RAR to individual SPARC galaxies

A&A 615, A3 (2018); arXiv:1803.00022. Read in full (method, results, §5, Table 2), 21 Nov 2026.

## What they did
- Fit the radial acceleration relation g = g_bar / (1 − e^(−√(g_bar/g†))), g† = 1.20 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s², to each of the 175 SPARC galaxies.
- Per galaxy: Υ_disk, Υ_bulge, distance D, inclination i. Gaussian priors: Υ_disk = 0.5 and Υ_bulge = 0.7 with 0.1 dex; D and i about the SPARC values with SPARC errors. Gas mass-to-light fixed (1.33 for helium is in Vgas).
- Distance: R ∝ D, baryon V ∝ √D, so g_bar is distance-free and g_obs ∝ 1/D. Inclination: V_obs and δV_obs ∝ sin i₀ / sin i.
- Likelihood on g_obs, σ = 2 V_obs δV_obs / R. emcee, 200 walkers; report the maximum-posterior set and χ²ν = χ²/(N − f).

## Results
- Most fits acceptable; ~10% genuinely poor (mostly Q = 3; some Q = 1–2: D631-7, F571-8, IC 4202, CamB, DDO 168, NGC 2915). χ²ν above 1 common, attributed to underestimated errors and non-circular motions.
- Median Υ_disk 0.50, Υ_bulge 0.67. D and i rarely moved beyond their errors (Q = 3 galaxies prefer smaller D, i).
- Residual scatter: selection i ≥ 30°, Q < 3, δV/V < 0.1 → 153 galaxies, 2694 points; rms 0.057 dex. Double-Gaussian residuals (0.062 and 0.020 dex).
- BTFR with fitted parameters: slope 3.79 ± 0.05, intrinsic scatter 0.035 dex (< 0.1 dex).
- §5, does g† vary? Refit with g† free (flat prior 0–10⁻⁹, or Gaussian 1.20 ± 0.02). Freeing it does not improve the fits: χ²ν distributions nearly identical; rms 0.054 (flat) vs 0.057 (Gaussian). The broad spread of free g† values under the flat prior is degeneracy with Υ and the nuisance parameters. Conclusion: consistent with one g† for all galaxies.
