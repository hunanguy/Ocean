# Hawking 1975 — Particle creation by black holes

**Citation.** S. W. Hawking, "Particle creation by black holes", Commun. Math. Phys. 43, 199–220 (1975); erratum 46, 206 (1976); doi:10.1007/BF02345020. Read 11 November 2026 from a copy of the published paper (jgluza.us.edu.pl/QM/CMPhawking.pdf) through the fetch tool's extraction: abstract and the passages below. Not read page by page.

**What the paper says.**
- Abstract: "In the classical theory black holes can only absorb and not emit particles." Quantum effects make them emit "at just the rate that one would expect if the black hole were an ordinary body" with temperature κ/2π (geometric units); "This thermal emission leads to a slow decrease in the mass of the black hole."
- The late-time outgoing wave packets "come from very high frequencies" in the incoming modes; the asymptotic result does not depend on the details of the collapse.
- Section 4: a negative energy flux into the hole makes the horizon's area decrease; that flux is not observable locally.
- The pair picture (a positive-energy member escaping, a negative-energy member crossing in) is called "heuristic only"; particle creation is a global process. A free-falling observer "would not see an infinite number of particles" coming from the collapsing body.
