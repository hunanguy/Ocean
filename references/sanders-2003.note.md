# Sanders 2003, clusters of galaxies with MOND — verification note

**Citation.** R. H. Sanders, "Clusters of galaxies with modified Newtonian dynamics (MOND)", Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 342, 901 (2003); arXiv:astro-ph/0212293. The journal reference was from recollection on 26 October; confirmed 22 November 2026 from the reference list of Skordis & Złośnik 2021 (their ref. 109, read). Read 26 October 2026 via alphaXiv: abstract, §1–4 with Table 1 and Figs. 1–7 (pp. 1–7), §6 (p. 9); read again in full 22 November 2026.

**Cited by.** v3.11 working list, S38. 3.16: preface and §13.3 "Galaxy clusters", §14 (bibitem sanders2003).

**What the paper gives.**
1. For 42 X-ray clusters (β-model parameters and temperatures from Reiprich 2001, h = 0.7), MOND still needs extra unseen mass. Two-component fits give the dark mass Md alongside the gas mass Mg (Table 1). The paper quotes Md/Mg = 1.60 ± 1.7 for MOND and 7.14 ± 2.7 for Newtonian dynamics. The extra mass sits mainly within about two gas core radii, though it "does extend beyond the core radius".
2. In MOND the discrepancy is "a factor of 2 to 3", against about 10 with Newtonian dynamics.
3. The largest discrepancies are in clusters with large core radii and low central densities, which have no cooling flow.
4. Stars are about 10¹³ M☉ within 1 Mpc in Coma (from The & White 1988), small beside the gas; Coma's MOND mass is about four times its gas mass.
5. Caveat: in the two-component model the dark mass is tied to the core radius, as Md = (16π/3) Σd rc², so its correlation with rc is partly built in.
6. Sanders suggests 2 eV neutrinos as the remedy (not ours).

**Bearing on the Ocean.** After the hot gas is counted, clusters still carry about twice the turning the galaxy-scale law gives. 26 Oct reading: that is a large eddy's unfilled capacity, largest in disturbed clusters (S38). 22 Nov (Neon and Fable validations of 3.16): far out the eddy of Eq. (15) turns travel as √(g_N g†), as MOND does, so it likely inherits this shortfall; "Galaxy clusters" added to what would kill it. The eddies of a cluster have not been computed.

**Status.** Read and verified 26 October 2026; journal reference confirmed 22 November 2026. Our recomputed mean of Md/Mg over the table is 2.32 (median 1.40); the paper's 1.60 may use a different weighting.
