# Garat & Price 2000, no conformally flat slices of Kerr — verification note

**Citation.** A. Garat and R. H. Price, "Nonexistence of conformally flat slices of the Kerr spacetime", arXiv:gr-qc/0002013 (2 February 2000); published in Physical Review D 61, 124011 (2000); DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.61.124011 (confirmed from the arXiv record, 26 October 2026). Read in full 26 October 2026 via alphaXiv (5 pages).

**Cited in 3.12.** §11.8 (no axisymmetric slicing of the spinning hole that reduces to the non-spinning case keeps the rulers shrunk equally beyond first order in spin; at first order no obstruction).

**What the paper gives.**
1. Question: is there a slicing of the spinning-hole (Kerr) geometry whose 3-geometry is conformally flat, i.e. rulers shrunk equally in every direction?
2. Restrictions: axisymmetric slices, of the form t = aF(r, θ) + O(a²), that go smoothly to slices of constant Schwarzschild time as the spin a → 0.
3. Test: the Bach (Cotton–York) tensor, whose vanishing "is a necessary and sufficient condition for a 3-geometry to be conformally flat" (Eq. 1).
4. Because the time–azimuth part of the Kerr metric is proportional to a, the slice's 3-geometry differs from a constant-time Schwarzschild slice only at order a² (Eqs. 9–13). So the Bach tensor vanishes at first order in a: at first order in spin there is no obstruction.
5. At order a², no slicing function F makes the Bach tensor vanish (Eqs. 14–28). Conclusion: under the stated restrictions, "there is no slicing of the Kerr spacetime that is conformally flat."
6. Limits stated by the authors: not ruled out are isolated values of a, families not reaching a = 0, non-axisymmetric slicings, and families reducing as a → 0 to slicings t = G(r, θ) other than constant time.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** 3.10's two principles (§11.7) are written on rulers shrunk equally in every direction (ψ⁴ times flat). Under the paper's restrictions, they cannot describe a spinning hole beyond first order in spin; at first order there is no obstruction. Item 1 therefore closes at first order, with second order carried to items 5 and 6.
