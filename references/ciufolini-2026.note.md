**Cited in 3.12.** §11.8 (LARES-2: 1.001 ± 0.001 (stat) ± 0.002 (syst) of General Relativity's value).

**What the table gives.**
1. (a) Frame-dragging nodal drift of the LARES-2 + LAGEOS combination over 14 days: 2.352 ± 0.0019 mas (t = 1238). Converted: 61.36 mas/yr, matching the GR prediction of about 61.36 mas/yr for the combination (Ciufolini et al. 2023). Residual K₁ tide amplitude −13.54 ± 0.21 mas, fitted and removed.
2. (b) Error budget, each as a fraction of frame dragging: LARES-2 injection errors negligible; non-zonal harmonics 0.1%; tides 0.1%; albedo 0.1%; thermal drag and eclipses 0.1%; measurement error of the orbital parameters 0.1%. Total root-sum-squared 0.2%.
3. (c) Result: μ = 1.001 ± 0.001 (statistical) ± 0.002 (systematic), with μ = 1 the GR value (GEODYN II analysis). Monte Carlo simulation (2025): 1.0001 ± 0.0019. Covariance analysis (2017): 1.0007 ± 0.0019.
4. From the article page and press release: about 200,000 laser-ranging measurements, July 2022 to June 2025 (about 1,050 days, one nodal period); the result bounds Chern–Simons gravity, |Ω̇_CS/Ω̇_GR| ≲ 0.002.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** Frame dragging near Earth is measured at General Relativity's strength to 0.2%, the sharpest such test (supersedes Ciufolini et al. 2019, 2%). Any Ocean account of frame dragging must give the same strength from Earth's whole spin. The 26 October restart of item 1 obtains it at first order from inside the Ocean, conditional on stated assumptions (item 1 note, §§4–6).
