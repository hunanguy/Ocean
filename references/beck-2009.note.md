# Beck 2009 — Axiomatic approach to the cosmological constant

Physica A 388, 3384–3390 (2009). arXiv:0810.0752 (v1 4 Oct 2008, v2 23 Oct 2008). Read 15 Nov 2026 (Opus 5.5, via the arXiv abstract and the PDF's text of Eqs. 1–2).

**What it reports (theirs).** Beck sets out four axioms for a cosmological constant, by analogy with the Khinchin axioms that fix the Shannon entropy: it should depend only on fundamental constants, be bounded below (Λ > 0), and depend on them in the simplest way. Evaluating them gives Λ = (G²/ħ⁴)(m_e/α_el)⁶ (Eq. 1), with α_el the low-energy fine structure constant, and a vacuum energy density ρ_vac = (c⁴/8πG)Λ (Eq. 2, equation of state w = −1). He reports it in agreement with WMAP data, and reads it as giving physical meaning to the Eddington–Dirac large-number hypothesis.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** This is the relation of §10.3 (Λ = G²m_e⁶/(α⁶ħ⁴) ≈ 1.36 × 10⁻⁵² m⁻²), which Chavanis 2026 recalls without deriving, and, rearranged, §14's G = α³ħ²√Λ/m_e³. Cited in 3.15.1 for attribution only: it answers §14's request to trace the relation to the source that derived it. It is not support for the Ocean; whether the Ocean produces the relation or it is a coincidence of the present epoch stays open (§14, "The strength of gravity").
