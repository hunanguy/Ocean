**Claims, and what the paper says.**

1. *Sound in a flowing fluid sees an effective Lorentzian metric built from density, flow and sound speed.* — Supported, for a barotropic, inviscid, irrotational fluid: ds² = (ρ/c)[−c²dt² + (dx − v dt)²]. Non-relativistic derivation.
2. *Sound rays depend only on c and v, not on the conformal factor* (light bending depends on c_s alone). — Supported: null geodesics are insensitive to the conformal factor; in the eikonal limit propagation depends only on local sound speed and flow.
3. *The acoustic metric is related to matter algebraically, not by Einstein's equations.* — Supported.
4. *N3's relativistic formula* g^eff = Ω²[g + (1 − c_s²)uu] — **not in this paper**, which is non-relativistic. Cite Babichev, Mukhanov & Vikman 2008 for the relativistic form.

**Two points that bear on 3.9.**
- *An acoustic metric cannot equal Schwarzschild exactly, only up to a conformal factor* (§7), because continuity constrains the flow. For S1: a geometry built from c_s and the flow alone fixes the light cones; the conformal factor, which governs slow patterns, must come from how patterns scale (W1). Add one sentence to S1.
- *A superfluid with a normal component has two acoustic metrics, first and second sound* (footnote 3, citing Comer). The one-speed principle (U1) is in tension with 3.8's requirement of a normal component for mutual friction (V1, R23). New open item for 3.9.
