# Doran — A new form of the Kerr solution
arXiv:gr-qc/9910099v3 (31 Jan 2000). Read in full 22 Nov 2026. Journal reference (Phys. Rev. D) not confirmed from a read source; cited by arXiv number.

**What it says that we use.** The Schwarzschild form ds² = dt² − (dr + √(2M/r) dt)² − r²dΩ² (Painlevé–Gullstrand; eq. 1), in which t is the proper time of observers falling radially from rest at infinity, extends to Kerr (eq. 18): the time coordinate "represents the local proper time for free-falling observers" on trajectories of constant θ, φ "and zero velocity at infinity" (eq. 22), and the metric is "well behaved at the horizon" (valid inside and outside). The transformation from advanced Eddington–Finkelstein form is eqs. (20)–(21), with √(2Mr(r² + a²)).

**Use.** §11.8 (3.16): the phase θ = μ∞[t + F(r)] with ΔF′ = √(2Mr(r² + a²)) gives exactly Doran's slices (turning per moment made exactly μ∞; regular across both edges). Cited for attribution (Neon and Fable, 22 Nov: the identity is exact, not only numerical).
