# Lisanti, Moschella, Outmezguine & Slone 2019, local Milky Way tests of MOND-like forces — verification note

**Citation.** M. Lisanti, M. Moschella, N. J. Outmezguine and O. Slone, "Testing Dark Matter and Modifications to Gravity using Local Milky Way Observables", Phys. Rev. D 100, 083009 (2019); arXiv:1812.08169 (v2, 12 September 2019). Read 21 November 2026 via alphaXiv: pp. 1, 5–6, 11–13. Cited in the paper as lisanti2019.

**Used by.** §6, §10.4(2), §14; item 2 of the 3.16 list.

**What the paper gives.**
1. A framework for testing any account against local Milky Way data: baryons as a Hernquist bulge (0.6 kpc) plus double-exponential stellar and gas disks; the local rotation speed and slope (Eilers et al. 2019); the local stellar and gas surface densities within 1.1 kpc (McKee et al. 2015); the vertical dispersions and densities of Zhang et al.'s three K-dwarf populations, through the vertical Jeans equation. R₀ = 8.122 kpc.
2. A force that scales up the baryons' field without turning it ("scalar amplification") is disfavored against a dark-matter halo, because the data want a large radial enhancement with little vertical; ΔBIC 4.1 for a free linear interpolation function, 4.8–10.4 for specific ones (10.4 for the radial acceleration relation's).
3. Local dark-matter density 0.29 ± 0.06 GeV/cm³ in the halo fit. No code or data tables are published with the paper.

**Status.** Read and verified 21 November 2026.
