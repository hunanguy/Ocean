# Pantheon+ (Scolnic et al. 2022; Brout et al. 2022)

**Citation.** D. Scolnic et al., "The Pantheon+ Analysis: The Full Dataset and Light-Curve Release" (2022), arXiv:2112.03863; D. Brout et al., "The Pantheon+ Analysis: Cosmological Constraints" (2022), arXiv:2202.04077. Data release: github.com/PantheonPlusSH0ES/DataRelease (Keith pointed to it, 22 Nov 2026 21:32). Used for the dynamo-output work (`claude/dynamo-output.md`). Read 22 Nov 2026 21:59 from the arXiv abstract pages (for the 3.16 citation): Scolnic et al. release 1,701 light curves of 1,550 spectroscopically confirmed type Ia supernovae from 18 surveys (doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ac8b7a); Brout et al. analyse them over redshifts 0.001 to 2.26, finding Ω_M = 0.334 ± 0.018 from supernovae alone in flat ΛCDM and, with the microwave background and BAO, w0 = −0.978 (+0.024/−0.031), consistent with a cosmological constant (doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ac8e04). Journal volume and page not confirmed from a read source; the paper cites doi and arXiv. Cited in the 3.16 draft, §5.1, as scolnic2022 and brout2022.

**Files used.** `Pantheon+_Data/4_DISTANCES_AND_COVAR/Pantheon+SH0ES.dat` (1,701 light curves; columns include zHD, zHEL, m_b_corr, MU_SH0ES, IS_CALIBRATOR) and `Pantheon+SH0ES_STAT+SYS.cov` (first line N = 1701, then the N × N statistical plus systematic covariance in magnitudes). Fetched with git (sparse checkout); a plain download from the workspace is refused.

**How used.** m_b_corr with the full covariance; zHD > 0.01, which leaves 1,590 and removes the Cepheid hosts; luminosity distance (1 + zHEL) D_M(zHD); the supernovae's absolute magnitude free and removed analytically, so H₀ does not enter. Held-fixed (flat, w = −1) fit together with DESI DR2 BAO: Ω_m = 0.304, supernova χ² 1405 for 1,590.
