**Claims, and what the paper says.**

1. *The generalized Chaplygin gas is p = −A/ρ^α, interpolating between dust and a cosmological constant.* — Supported, with ρ = (A + B/a^(3(1+α)))^(1/(1+α)) and an intermediate phase p = αρ.
2. *Exponent range.* — The paper considers **0 < α ≤ 1** only. W2's α = 3 lies outside the range studied here. The proposal must say so rather than call W2's law "a generalized Chaplygin gas" without qualification.
3. *Origin.* — The model is derived from a complex scalar field in the Thomas–Fermi limit, Φ ∝ φ e^(−imθ): the same field content as 3.8's Ocean. Worth noting as a structural kinship.
