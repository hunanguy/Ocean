# Martinis & Perković 2010 — reading note

**Citation.** M. Martinis and N. Perković, "Is exponential metric a natural space-time metric of Newtonian gravity?", arXiv:1009.6017 (2010); journal publication not confirmed. Read in full 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv (7 pp.). Full text not stored.

**Cited in 3.12.** Not cited. §11.6 cites Arminjon 2006 for the exponential metric.

**What it gives.** The isotropic exponential metric g₀₀ = e^(−2u), g_ii = −e^(2u), derived from Newtonian gravity with energy-dependent masses; it reproduces perihelion precession and the full light deflection. Attributes the metric to Yilmaz (Phys. Rev. 111, 1417 (1958)) and notes the sharp criticism of Yilmaz's theory (Misner 1999; Ibison 2006), including that it predicts no black holes.

**Use.** Context for W2's multiplicative composition. The version to cite is Arminjon 2006, which arrives at the same metric within a worked-out theory. Not cited.
