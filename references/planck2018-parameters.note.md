# Planck 2018 results VI — cosmological parameters
Planck Collaboration (N. Aghanim et al.), *Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters*, Astron. Astrophys. 641, A6 (2020); arXiv:1807.06209 (v4, 9 Aug 2021). Journal reference confirmed from the reference list of Skordis & Złośnik 2021 (their ref. 101, read). Read 22 November 2026: abstract, §3 (pp. 14–16, 19), Tables 1–2.

**What it gives that we use.**
- Abstract and §3.5, Eqs. (23)–(24): dark matter density Ω_c h² = 0.1200 ± 0.0012 and baryon density Ω_b h² = 0.02237 ± 0.00015 (TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing); abstract rounds to 0.120 ± 0.001 and 0.0224 ± 0.0001. Ratio 5.36. "The matter mostly consists of cold dark matter, with density constrained at the percent level."
- §3.5: the baryon density modifies "the relative heights of the even and odd acoustic peaks"; the matter density from the scale dependence of the amplitude and the lensing.
- Table 2: last scattering z* = 1089.92 ± 0.25; matter–radiation equality z_eq = 3402 ± 26; H₀ = 67.36 ± 0.54; Ω_m = 0.3153 ± 0.0073; age 13.797 ± 0.023 Gyr (all base ΛCDM, model-dependent where stated).
- Abstract: w₀ = −1.03 ± 0.03 with BAO and supernovae, "consistent with a cosmological constant".
