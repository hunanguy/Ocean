# van Dokkum, Danieli, Abraham, Conroy & Romanowsky 2019 — A second galaxy missing dark matter in the NGC 1052 group

ApJ Letters 874, L5 (2019). arXiv:1901.05973. Read 11 Nov 2026 (Opus 5.5, via WebFetch summary of the arXiv PDF).

**What it reports (theirs).** NGC 1052-DF4, an ultra-diffuse galaxy at 19.9 ± 2.8 Mpc (surface brightness fluctuations; 20 Mpc adopted), stellar mass (1.5 ± 0.4) × 10⁸ M☉, R_e = 1.6 kpc. Seven globular clusters: observed rms 5.8 km/s; intrinsic dispersion 4.2 (+4.4/−2.2) km/s (ABC) or 3.8 (+3.4/−2.6) km/s (likelihood). Expected from the stars alone about 7.3 km/s; with a standard (NFW) halo about 30 km/s. Tracer mass 0.4 (+1.2/−0.3) × 10⁸ M☉ within 7 kpc, at or below the stellar mass: "cannot reject the hypothesis that there is no dark matter." Compared with DF2 (stellar dispersion 8.5 km/s, Danieli et al. 2019). Notes tension with MOND predictions (Müller, Famaey & Zhao 2019). Formation unexplained; tidal origin "perhaps the most plausible" but with problems (low metallicity, no debris).

**Bearing on the Ocean.** Observed case of bound matter with no extra pull: what §6's candidate calls bound matter that no river passes, sitting in the current directly. Supportive; cite for the observation only, not for their reading.
