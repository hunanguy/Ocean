# Brout, Massar, Parentani & Spindel 1995 — Hawking radiation without trans-Planckian frequencies

**Citation.** R. Brout, S. Massar, R. Parentani and Ph. Spindel, "Hawking radiation without trans-Planckian frequencies", Phys. Rev. D 52, 4559 (1995); doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.52.4559 (arXiv record); arXiv:hep-th/9506121. Read 11 November 2026 via alphaXiv: abstract, §§1–7, added note.

**What the paper says.**
- Analytic account of Unruh's 1995 numerical result. In momentum space near a horizon the wave equation becomes first order; the in-vacuum means in-modes carry positive momentum only. Both survive truncating the spectrum, and suffice for the thermal flux: |β/α| = e^{−4πMω}, T = 1/8πM (their Eq. 19).
- With a truncation of the energy at the Planck scale in the free-fall frame (their Eq. 26, g(p)), waves no longer approach the horizon within less than a Planck length; "fluctuations within this skin steadily develop into outgoing pairs" — a steady conversion of modes in a Planckian skin into the usual outgoing modes. Unruh vacuum "is still a valid concept just outside a Planckian skin of the horizon".
- The radiation stays a pair phenomenon: each outgoing quantum has a partner across the horizon.
- Conjecture: Hawking radiation "is protected from the vicissitudes of quantum gravity"; "an essentially kinematic response to the presence of the event horizon".
