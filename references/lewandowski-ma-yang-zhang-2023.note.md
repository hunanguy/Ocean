# Lewandowski, Ma, Yang & Zhang 2023, "Quantum Oppenheimer–Snyder and Swiss Cheese Models" — verification note

**Citation.** J. Lewandowski, Y. Ma, J. Yang and C. Zhang, "Quantum Oppenheimer–Snyder and Swiss Cheese Models", Phys. Rev. Lett. 130 (2023) 101501; arXiv:2210.02253. Journal reference confirmed (FAU research record).

**Read.** 6 Nov 2026 via alphaXiv, in full (8 pp. with appendix).

**What the paper gives.**
1. A ball of dust collapsing under the loop-quantum-cosmology equation H² = (8πG/3)ρ(1 − ρ/ρ_c) (their Eq. 2, ρ_c = √3/(32π²γ³G²ħ)), glued to a static outside: the ball falls through the point of no return, reaches its least radius r_b = (αGM/2)^{1/3}, bounces and expands; the outside is Schwarzschild deformed by αG²M²/r⁴, with an inner horizon.
2. A least mass for a black hole, of order the Planck mass; an outside observer never sees the bounce.
3. Any deformed equation H² = (8πG/3)ρX(ρ) gives the outside F = G = (2GM/r) X(3M/4πr³).

**Bearing on the Ocean.** The paper's falling-ball turnaround times (10⁻⁴ s, 28 s, 12 h; `claude/core_bounce.py`) use this same construction: a ball falling from rest far away (Oppenheimer–Snyder form) under the borrowed equation. Precedent for the construction, not support for the Ocean's mechanism.
