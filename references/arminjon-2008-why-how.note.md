**What it gives.**
1. *A review of Lorentz–Poincaré ether models of gravity:* Podlaha & Sjödin (ether density increases toward the mass and acts as the refractive index for light; clocks slow and rods contract at rest in the ether); Sjödin's Lagrangian; Broekaert's Hamiltonian reproducing the four classical tests at 1PN; Winterberg (a flowing substratum whose motion fixes rod contraction and clock dilation); crystal-substratum and condensate models.
2. *Why the sound speed must equal c* (§3.3): since particles are flows in the ether, the ether's sound speed is their speed limit, and special relativity requires that limit to be c, so p_e = c²ρ_e. A direct precedent for U1's "matter's speed limit is the medium's carrying speed", with the difference that in Arminjon that speed is fixed while the Ocean's varies with the state.
3. *Status of v1:* exact Schwarzschild exterior; light as in GR at 1PN; binary-pulsar energy loss of GR's order; bounce instead of a singularity in collapse; accelerated expansion predicted; celestial mechanics residuals against an ephemeris; WEP violation (cured in v2).
