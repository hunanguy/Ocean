# Biskupek, Müller & Torre 2021 — verification note

**Citation.** L. Biskupek, J. Müller and J.-M. Torre, "Benefit of new high-precision LLR data for the determination of relativistic parameters", Universe 7(2), 34 (2021); arXiv:2012.12032. Open access (MDPI). Read in full on 20 October 2026, from the arXiv preprint (v1, 22 December 2020).

**Cited by.** G1 (§7.6): G is a constant of the beat.

**What the paper does.** It analyses 27,485 lunar laser ranging normal points, April 1970 to April 2020, including many new infrared measurements from OCA. It fits relativistic parameters together with the Earth–Moon model, letting G vary in time as G(t) = G₀(1 + (Ġ/G₀)Δt + ½(G̈/G₀)Δt²).

**Claim our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *Lunar laser ranging finds no drift in G.* Supported.
   - Ġ/G₀ = (−5.0 ± 9.6) × 10⁻¹⁵ per year, estimated separately from G̈.
   - (0.2 ± 1.3) × 10⁻¹⁴ per year when estimated jointly with G̈.
   - The earlier Hofmann & Müller 2018 analysis gave (7.1 ± 7.6) × 10⁻¹⁴ per year.
   - The paper also lists other bounds: planetary ephemerides at 7 × 10⁻¹⁴ and 2 × 10⁻¹³ per year, and MESSENGER below 4 × 10⁻¹⁴ per year.
   - Conclusion: "no violation of Einstein's theory is found".
2. *Over the present expansion epoch.* This is our extrapolation, not the paper's claim. A drift of order 10⁻¹⁴ per year sustained over 13.8 billion years amounts to about one part in 10⁴. The measurement spans only fifty years, so the paper limits the present rate, not the whole history, and our text says "if the rate has been steady".

**Related points.**
- The paper notes that theories with preferred reference frames can allow G to vary in time (Will & Nordtvedt 1972). That bears directly on the Ocean, which has a rest frame.
- It reports equivalence-principle and PPN limits from the same data: Δ(m_g/m_i) = (−2.1 ± 2.4) × 10⁻¹⁴, β − 1 = (6.2 ± 7.2) × 10⁻⁵ and γ − 1 = (1.7 ± 1.6) × 10⁻⁴. These are relevant to the refractive reading's requirements in §2.5.
- It confirms the Hofmann & Müller 2018 citation: Class. Quantum Grav. 35, 035015.
- Williams, Turyshev & Boggs 2004 could not be retrieved, so this more recent analysis replaces it.
