**Cited in 3.12.** §10.2 (Arminjon's scalar ether theory); §11.6, The current through time (the exponential metric of Yilmaz, which Arminjon's isotropic theory has in full).

**What the theory is.** A preferred-frame, bimetric scalar theory. The medium ("ether") is the only substance and particles are organized flows in it. The scalar field β ≡ ρ_e/ρ_e^∞ ≤ 1 (local ether density over its value far from masses) dilates clock periods and contracts rods in the same ratio, now isotropically: g₀₀ = β², g_ij = β⁻² δ_ij. The field obeys the flat wave equation □ψ = 4πGσ/c² with β = e^(−ψ). The pressure–density law is p_e = c²ρ_e. Gravity is the "Archimedes' thrust" of the ether's pressure gradient, g = −c² ∇β/β. Motion follows an extension of Newton's second law; geodesic motion holds only in static fields, so it is not a metric theory in the PPN sense.

**Claims, and what the paper says.**
1. *Static spherical metric is the exponential metric.* — Supported: β = e^(−GM/c²R), so g₀₀ = e^(−2u), g_ij = e^(2u)δ_ij, u = GM/(c²R).
2. *1PN agreement with GR:* g₀₀ = 1 − 2u + 2u², g_ij = −(1 + 2u)δ_ij — the standard PN metric of GR (γ = β = 1 in effect). Light deflection, time delay and redshift are as in GR, also with the solar system moving through the preferred frame.
3. *Isotropy cures the WEP violation.* v1's direction-only contraction made extended bodies fall differently at the point-particle limit; the isotropic v2 removes it.
4. *No Lense–Thirring effect in the usual sense* (footnote 10).

**Bearing on 3.9.** The isotropic scaling of clocks and rods by one factor of the medium's state is W1's even split, reached by the same Lorentz–Poincaré reasoning. The exponential metric is W2's time part exactly. The lesson of v1 supports W1's isotropy. The missing frame dragging is a warning for W2 (new R35).
