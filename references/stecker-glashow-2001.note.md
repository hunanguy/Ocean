# Stecker & Glashow 2001 — verification note

**Citation.** F. W. Stecker and S. L. Glashow, "New tests of Lorentz invariance following from observations of the highest energy cosmic gamma rays", Astropart. Phys. 16, 97 (2001) (confirmed by Jacobson et al. 2003, ref. 14); arXiv:astro-ph/0102226. Read in full 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv (5 pp.). Full text not stored.

**Cited in 3.12.** §11.4 (electrons bound the difference between light's and matter's speed limits to about one part in 10¹⁵).

**What the paper gives.** With c_e = c_γ(1 + δ): for c_e > c_γ (matter's limit above light's, N4's case), Mrk 501 gamma rays to about 20 TeV give δ ≤ 1.3 × 10⁻¹⁵; for c_e < c_γ, Crab photons above 50 TeV give |δ| ≤ 2 × 10⁻¹⁶.

**Use.** Supports "electron and photon speed limits agree to about 10⁻¹⁵" for the electron specifically. The stronger species-general bound for N4 is Coleman & Glashow's 10⁻²³ from protons.

**Status.** Verified 21 October 2026.
