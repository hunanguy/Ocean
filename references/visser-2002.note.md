# Visser 2002, Sakharov's induced gravity: a modern perspective — verification note

**Citation.** M. Visser, "Sakharov's induced gravity: a modern perspective", Mod. Phys. Lett. A 17, 977 (2002); doi:10.1142/S0217732302006886; arXiv:gr-qc/0204062 (v1, 19 April 2002). Volume and page confirmed 30 October 2026 from the NASA ADS record (bibcode 2002MPLA...17..977V). Original: A. D. Sakharov, Sov. Phys. Dokl. 12 (1968) 1040; reprinted Gen. Rel. Grav. 32 (2000) 365 (as given in Visser's ref. 1; not read directly). Read 29 October 2026: pp. 1–17 (whole paper).

**Cited in 3.12.** §10.2 (inherited list); §12.7, Equation (37) and its footnote.

**What the paper gives.**
1. Sakharov's idea: gravity is not fundamental; it "emerges from quantum field theory in roughly the same sense that hydrodynamics or continuum elasticity theory emerges from molecular physics" (abstract). Take a geometry with no dynamics of its own, put the vacuum fluctuations of the fields on it, and their one-loop response automatically contains the cosmological-constant term, the Einstein–Hilbert term and curvature-squared terms (Eqs. 1–2).
2. With a short-distance cutoff κ: 1/G = 1/G₀ − (1/2π) str[k₁ κ² − ...] (Eq. 21). In Sakharov's version (no bare terms, one loop dominant, cutoff at the Planck scale), 1/G ≈ −(1/2π) str[k₁] κ², with str[k₁] of order 1 (Eq. 29). str is a weighted count over all field species, fermions with a minus sign; values of k₁ per species in Table 1 (e.g. minimally coupled scalar 1/6, conformally coupled scalar 0, Weyl spinor −1/6, massless spin 1 −2/3).
3. Costs stated: the cosmological constant comes out of order κ⁴ unless put in by hand (p. 7); observation requires it below ~10⁻¹²³ in Planck units (Eqs. 24–25). Pauli's finiteness conditions, cancellations among species, are needed to tame it; the Standard Model does not satisfy them.

**Use in 3.12.** The strength of gravity as the stiffness of the plexus, a sea of waves the dynamo sustains, against bending: set by the waves' shortest wavelength (the cost of a dent) and by a weighted count of the kinds. With fifteen builds per family, twelve carriers and a conformally coupled field giving mass, str[k₁] = −1/2: gravity attracts and the dent lies near 3.5 times the Planck energy. With sixteen per family the count gives no gravity.
