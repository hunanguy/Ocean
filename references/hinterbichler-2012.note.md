# Hinterbichler — Theoretical aspects of massive gravity
arXiv:1105.3735v2 (2 Oct 2011). Read 22 Nov 2026: Section 2 (pp. 13–16) and Section 6.1 (pp. 45–48). Journal reference (Rev. Mod. Phys.) not confirmed from a read source; cited by arXiv number.

**What it says that we use.** The free Fierz–Pauli action (2.1) "contains all possible contractions of two powers of h, with up to two derivatives." For m = 0 its four two-derivative terms "are chosen to match exactly those obtained by linearizing the Einstein-Hilbert action", and the gauge symmetry δh = ∂ξ + ∂ξ "fixes all the coefficients of the two-derivative part of (2.1), up to an overall coefficient." The action is credited to Fierz and Pauli, Proc. Roy. Soc. Lond. A 173, 211 (1939) [its ref. 28]. Section 6.1: expanding Einstein–Hilbert to second order gives exactly the m = 0 action; starting from the linear theory and asking for consistent interactions forces Einstein's (refs. 4–10 there).

**Use.** §11.5 (3.16): the Ocean's first-order law, four coefficients fixed up to scale by preservation, is this known result; cited for the attribution (Fable, 22 Nov). Fierz & Pauli named through this review, not read directly.
