# Hamilton & Lisle 2008 — verification note

**Citation.** A. J. S. Hamilton and J. P. Lisle, "The river model of black holes", Am. J. Phys. 76, 519 (2008); arXiv:gr-qc/0411060 (v2, 31 Aug 2006). Already cited in 3.8 §8.1 as hamilton2008 (from 3.7). Read 21 October 2026 via alphaXiv: abstract, §I–II (pp. 1–5), §III H–I and summary (pp. 11, 13), appendix (p. 15). Full text not stored; arXiv ID above.

**Cited by.** 3.8 §8.1 (river description; flat background has no observable meaning). For 3.9: U1 (flow unlimited), U3 (steepness, not speed), W2 note.

**Claims our text makes, and what the paper says.**

1. *The Schwarzschild geometry can be described as space flowing inward at the Newtonian escape velocity, reaching light speed at the horizon.* — Supported: Gullstrand–Painlevé form, river velocity √(2GM/r), horizon where it equals c; inside, the river outruns light.
2. *Objects move through the river by special relativity; the river itself can move faster than light* (U1: flow unlimited, disturbances limited relative to the local medium). — Supported: the river moves in Galilean fashion through the flat background; objects cannot move faster than light *through the river*.
3. *A uniform current is unfelt; what an object responds to is the tidal change of the river* (U3). — Supported: a particle comoving with the river sees no change; it is boosted only by tidal changes in river velocity along its path; tidal stretching follows from the river's inward acceleration.
4. *The flat background has no observable meaning* (3.8 §8.1). — Supported (§III I).
5. *Rotating holes: the river does not swirl but twists.* — Supported (Doran form; six-component river field).

**Related point.** The authors note the aether overtones are no more substantial than the picture of space expanding. Useful context for U1, not a claim to cite.
