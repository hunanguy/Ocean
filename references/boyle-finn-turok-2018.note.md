# Boyle, Finn & Turok — CPT-Symmetric Universe
arXiv:1803.08928v3 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 251301, 2018). Read: full Letter (29 Oct 2026).
- Proposal: the universe after the big bang is the CPT image of the universe before it; the two form a universe/anti-universe pair emerging from nothing into a hot radiation era.
- Builds on Stueckelberg (1941): an antiparticle, in the authors' terms, is a particle whose proper time runs counter to the embedding time; the contracting half is read as an anti-universe. The matter/antimatter asymmetry on one side of the bang is the opposite of the asymmetry on the other.
- Needs the standard model with right-handed neutrinos; one is stable (Z2) and is the dark matter, mass 4.8 × 10^8 GeV. The other two explain the observed asymmetry by thermal leptogenesis.
- Predictions: light neutrinos are Majorana (neutrinoless double-beta decay); the lightest neutrino is massless; no primordial long-wavelength gravitational waves.
- Use in 3.12 (§10.2, §12.4, §12.8): the closest precedent for antimatter as a pattern whose own time runs counter to ours. Differences from the Ocean: their mirror is across the bang (one global reversal), the Ocean's is the reclaiming side of every rebuild; they require the right-handed neutrino and dark matter, the Ocean has neither. Shared prediction: neutrinoless double-beta decay.
