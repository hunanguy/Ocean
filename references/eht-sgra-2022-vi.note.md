**What the paper finds.**
1. *The observed image size of Sgr A* is within about 10 per cent of the Kerr prediction*, using the stellar-dynamics mass-to-distance priors (Keck and VLTI) from the orbit of S0-2.
2. *Fractional deviation of the shadow size from Schwarzschild*, fiducial analyses (eht-imaging, GRMHD calibration): δ = −0.04 (+0.09, −0.10) with the Keck prior; δ = −0.08 ± 0.09 with the VLTI prior. All method combinations are consistent with each other and with no deviation (Table 2).
3. *Bounds on the shadow radius*, G = c = 1: 3√3(1 − 0.14)M ≲ r̃_sh ≲ 3√3(1 + 0.05)M, i.e. 4.5M–5.5M (Keck); 3√3(1 − 0.17)M ≲ r̃_sh ≲ 3√3(1 + 0.01)M, i.e. 4.3M–5.3M (VLTI) (eqs. 12–13).
4. *Spin and inclination*: the Kerr shadow size varies by about 8 per cent with spin and viewing angle; the size test constrains mainly the tt component of the metric and depends weakly on spin.
5. *Alternatives to a horizon*: a thermally re-emitting surface is ruled out and a fully reflecting surface is unlikely (§4); compact objects without a photon sphere are disfavored by the presence of a central brightness depression (§4.3).
6. *S0-2 precession*: f_SP = 1.1 ± 0.19, so 2γ − β = 1.3 ± 0.57 at S0-2's orbit (eqs. 24–26, citing Gravity Collaboration 2020).
7. *M87**: the M87 shadow posteriors (stellar- and gas-dynamics masses) are compared in Fig. 24 and are consistent with Kerr; not read in detail here.

**Bearing on the Ocean.**
- R12 (the Ocean's exterior for a non-spinning mass equals Schwarzschild): predicted δ = 0, shadow radius 3√3 M = 5.196M. Inside both fiducial bounds. Passes.
- The horizonless exponential geometry (OS2 taken all the way in), shadow radius 5.44M, δ = +0.046: inside the Keck bound (upper limit 5.46M) but outside the VLTI bound (upper limit 5.25M). Disfavored, not excluded at this precision; recorded for the 3.9 lineage only.
- Keith's position (a horizon is a point of no return, not a surface) is consistent with item 5: no surface.
- S0-2: the Ocean's γ = β = 1 gives f_SP = 1, consistent with 1.1 ± 0.19.
- Spin is not covered: R12 is for a non-spinning mass. Spinning holes need frame dragging (R35).
