# Everitt et al. 2011, Gravity Probe B final results — verification note

**Citation.** C. W. F. Everitt et al., "Gravity Probe B: Final Results of a Space Experiment to Test General Relativity", Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 221101 (2011); arXiv:1105.3456. Read in full 25 October 2026 via alphaXiv (full text). Full text not stored.

**Cited by.** 3.11 §11.8 (Gravity Probe B, opening and table). Working note: claude/v3.11-item1-frame-dragging.md.

**What the paper finds.**
1. Four cryogenic gyroscopes in a 642 km polar orbit, referenced to the guide star IM Pegasi; data 28 August 2004 to 14 August 2005.
2. Joint four-gyro result (Table II): geodetic drift −6601.8 ± 18.3 mas/yr against the GR prediction −6606.1; frame-dragging drift −37.2 ± 7.2 mas/yr against −39.2. Accuracy 0.28% (geodetic) and 19% (frame dragging).
3. Main difficulty: patch-effect torques, modelled with trapped-flux mapping; total uncertainty includes statistical and parameter-sensitivity terms (Table III).
4. GP-B measures the spin–spin (gyroscope) frame-dragging effect; laser-ranged satellites measure the spin–orbit (nodal) effect.

**Bearing on the Ocean.** The geodetic term tests γ = 1, which 3.10 takes from the measured bending of light. The frame-dragging term is the test for item 1: the 26 October rule from inside the Ocean gives 39.2 mas/yr projected on the guide star (item 1 note, §4), 0.3σ from the measurement.
