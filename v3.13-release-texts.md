## 2. GitHub release (archived by Zenodo)

**Tag:** `v3.13`

**Title:** `v3.13 — flow versus build, the plexus law and the eddy`

**Notes:**

Version 3.13 decides how the Ocean's behaviors divide between its flow and the rebuild. The rebuild supplies the Ocean's current as it expands, so there is no separate rate of the phase to set, and nothing moving through the Ocean feels drag: gravity is the build's uneven making of time, not a current slowed by drag.

The Ocean-wide waves that hold the rebuilt state, the plexus, are given a law of their own, built from twists alone and tested seven ways (new Section 12.9). Its frame part is the teleparallel equivalent of General Relativity; it carries light, the eleven other carriers, the field that joins the two hands, and matter as half-angle waves whose backward solution is antimatter; and it allows exactly three families. Around a spinning black hole the same law sets the Ocean's rulers by direction, so the hole's outside is General Relativity's.

Section 6 is rewritten. A galaxy sits in an eddy of the Ocean far larger than itself, which acts through its own energy rather than a swirl in the disk's plane. Its strength is set by the galaxy's mass and one limit held at every eddy's center, giving the baryonic Tully–Fisher and radial acceleration relations; the limit has not followed the slowing of the expansion over eleven billion years. Appendix B follows the Milky Way's eddy, which carries the Earth and holds the black hole at its center, beside the Earth's rebuild and Appendix A's spinning black hole.

The preface gains an epigraph and the tenth revision, and every section has been brought into line with these decisions. Validated by an independent reading before release.
